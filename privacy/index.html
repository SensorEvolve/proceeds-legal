Proceeds privacy policy

Proceeds - Store Analytics ("Proceeds") is published by Mailogic AB, Sweden ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Summary

Proceeds does not collect personal data. Your App Store Connect API key and your sales reports stay on your iPhone. We run no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and we never see your sales numbers.

What stays on your device

  • Your API key (Issuer ID, Key ID, vendor number and private key) is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, only on that device. It is never synced to iCloud and never sent to us.
  • Your sales and subscription reports are downloaded directly from Apple and stored in the app's container on your iPhone, so the app and its widgets work offline.
  • Settings such as your display currency.

Deleting the app, or using Settings > Delete all data in the app, removes all of it.

Which services the app contacts

The app connects directly from your iPhone to these services. None of them receives your API key except Apple, and none receives your sales data.

Service Why What it receives
Apple App Store Connect API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com) Download your sales and subscription reports Requests signed with your API key
Apple iTunes Search (itunes.apple.com, mzstatic.com) Show your apps' icons The public App Store ID of each of your apps
Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) and currency-api via jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) Daily exchange rates to show totals in one currency Only dates; no identifiers
Apple App Store Purchases and subscriptions Handled entirely by Apple

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. We have no access to their logs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Children

Proceeds is a tool for app developers and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All data is on your device and under your control. For questions, contact sensorevolve@gmail.com.
