1. What Proceeds does

Proceeds shows sales, proceeds, downloads and subscription figures from reports you download from Apple with your own App Store Connect API key.

2. Data comes from Apple; totals are estimates

All figures come from Apple's reports. Apple publishes daily reports with a delay and may correct them later. Totals in your display currency are converted with public daily exchange rates and are estimates, marked with "≈". They are not your payout amounts. For official figures, use App Store Connect.

3. Dependence on Apple

Proceeds relies on Apple's App Store Connect API. If Apple changes, restricts or discontinues it, features may stop working until the app is updated, or permanently. We will try to keep Proceeds working but cannot guarantee that any feature remains available.

4. Your API key

You are responsible for the API key you add, including its access role and revoking it in App Store Connect when you no longer use it. Proceeds stores it only on your device (see the privacy policy).

5. Proceeds Pro subscription

  • Proceeds Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription, with a free trial when offered.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or when the free trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • You can manage and cancel the subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
